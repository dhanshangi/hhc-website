WHO WE ARE

Hello, we're
HASHHACKCODE.

We're passionate about making to everyone. Our team of dedicated educators, mentors, and coding enthusiasts believes that technology should be open to all—regardless of background, age, or gender.

We champion equitable education for learners of all cognitive abilities. Our programs are designed to support diverse learning styles, ensuring every student has the chance to excel and innovate in tech.

OUR STORY

In 2019, HashHackCode started with a simple mission: make tech education accessible to everyone. Our founder, Manu Sekar, saw how traditional learning often leaves out those with diverse cognitive abilities.

We're here to change that. Whether you're autistic, a senior citizen, or have different abilities, coding can open doors to independence and opportunity. Technology should enable, not exclude—and we're here to make that happen.

  • 01

    Journey for Inclusion

    In 2019, fueled by a commitment to empower women from underserved communities, we expanded our mission to include neurodiverse individuals, starting with just three students. This marked the beginning of our transformative journey from Chennai to a global impact.

  • 02

    The Digital Shift

    The 2020 pandemic prompted a swift move to online learning, broadening our educational reach and showcasing the adaptability of our neurodiverse learners. This transition underscored our commitment to making education accessible everywhere.

  • 03

    The COVID-19 Data Initiative

    In 2021, our graduates made COVID-19 data accessible, creating a website that catered to all, including those with visual impairments. This initiative highlighted the importance of inclusive information technology.

  • 04

    NFTs and the Metaverse

    Our students ventured into the Metaverse and NFTs, challenging stereotypes and demonstrating the creative potential within the neurodiverse community, pushing the boundaries of digital art and technology.

  • 05

    From Learners to Mentors

    By 2022, our graduates became mentors, embodying the transformative power of our educational approach by sharing their knowledge at events like Google Next, showcasing the success of full-circle learning.

  • 06

    The First Graduation

    We celebrated our first graduation ceremony, marking a significant achievement for 40 graduates who are now poised for promising futures, thanks to their newfound coding skills. This celebration underscored the transformative impact of our mission.

  • 07

    Towards Inclusive Workplaces

    Launching 'Cherized' in 2023, we furthered our expanded our commitment to workplace inclusivity, welcoming senior citizens, individuals with disabilities, and others into the world of coding. We believe tech careers should be accessible to everyone.

  • 08

    Showcasing our Neurodiverse Coders

    Our students developed an app using the Gemini API at Google I/O 2024, demonstrating their unique talents and furthering our commitment to inclusion in tech.

  • 09

    Expanding Our Reach with CodeLab

    In 2024, we partnered with inclusive schools and organizations to offer in-person group classes across the country, utilizing the learning app developed along with our neurodiverse coders.
